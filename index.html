FAQ

Questions answered.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

You can deposit funds directly through the bot using UPI, Cryptocurrency, or Binance Pay. Navigate to the Deposit section inside the bot and select your preferred payment method. Funds are typically reflected within minutes.
Yes, UPI is fully supported and is one of the most convenient ways to deposit for users in India. Instructions are provided within the bot after selecting UPI as your deposit method.
Cryptocurrency deposits are supported, including via Binance Pay. This provides a fast, borderless deposit option for international users. Select Crypto or Binance Pay from the deposit menu inside the bot.
Orders begin processing immediately after placement. Most orders start within minutes. Processing time varies depending on the service type and quantity, but the automated system handles execution as fast as possible.
